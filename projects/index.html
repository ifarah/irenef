projects

My research is organized in three interconnected areas, governance and informality, food, and methods, which inform and build upon one another.

governance and informality

Street vending associations and state formation in Mexico City

Street vending ordinance in San Francisco's Mission District

Vendors, community organizations, and street level bureaucrats respond to San Francisco's street vending ordinance.

Mobility of informal workers in Mexico City

Using location based services data to trace the daily mobility of street market vendors.

food

Informal outlets and the retail food environment in Mexico

Where Mexican households buy food, and why food policy must account for informal outlets.

methods

Enumeration of street vendors through satellite images

How sensitive are walking access metrics to their parameters? Twelve models compared across six cities.

Teaching critical spatial data science

Strategies for teaching critical GIS together with technical mapping methods.
